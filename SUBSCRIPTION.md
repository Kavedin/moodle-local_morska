# Morska Accessibility Suite — subscription model

Morska Accessibility Suite is offered as a **paid institutional subscription product**.

## Trial

A new Moodle installation receives one server-registered **15-day trial**. No licence key is required during the trial. Trial entitlement is validated against the KTC licensing service so reinstalling the plugin on the same registered Moodle site does not create a new trial.

## Paid subscription

After the trial ends, learner-facing Morska services require an active Morska subscription entitlement. Institutions can subscribe at:

https://ktc.co.ug/downloads/morska/

The subscription may include, according to the purchased plan, official Morska builds, continued entitlement to subscription-gated functionality, updates, Moodle compatibility releases, H5P and SCORM improvements, documentation, licence management, and support.
