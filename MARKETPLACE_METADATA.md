# Moodle Marketplace listing draft

## Product type
Paid local plugin / annual institutional subscription.

## Name
Morska Accessibility Suite

## Frankenstyle name
`local_morska`

## Short description
Morska extends accessibility support across Moodle course pages and compatible H5P and SCORM content through text-to-speech, keyboard navigation, auto-highlight, auto-scroll, visual reading tools, and selected interactive-content assistance.

## Commercial disclosure
Morska is a paid subscription product. New installations receive a server-registered 15-day trial with no licence key required. After the trial, learner-facing Morska services require an active subscription entitlement. Purchase and subscription information: https://ktc.co.ug/downloads/morska/

## External service
The plugin communicates with the KTC licensing service at `ktc.co.ug` for trial registration and subscription validation. The transmitted fields are declared through Moodle's Privacy API. Learner names, email addresses, grades, course content, and learner activity records are not transmitted for licence validation.
